Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my active order in real time?

Once your order is placed, you can visit the Orders page or click Track Live Status to view the live animated status timeline and real-time courier GPS route.

What should I do if an item is missing or incorrect?

We offer a 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. Simply use the Live Chat box on the right or tap "Contact Support" on your order details page, and our team will issue an instant refund or replacement credit.

How do I apply a discount promo code?

In your Cart or Checkout page, type your promo code (such as WELCOME50 or CRAVE20) into the promo code box and click Apply. Your discount will be calculated instantly.

How do I switch between Customer, Restaurant, and Courier modes?

Use the Demo Mode Banner at the top of the screen to switch experiences in 1-click between Customer, Restaurant Kitchen Partner, and Courier Delivery Partner.
